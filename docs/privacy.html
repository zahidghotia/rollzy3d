Rollzy 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Rollzy 3D ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version of the game.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: coins, unlocked levels and stars, the balls and trails you own, daily reward and spin timers, and settings such as sound and vibration. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any account login.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data related to ads.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when you first open the game, you are asked whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the game's Settings screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).
  • Optional ads: most ads in the game are optional "watch an ad for a reward" offers. You can play without choosing them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept and protected according to Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

See also our Terms of Service.

Contact
